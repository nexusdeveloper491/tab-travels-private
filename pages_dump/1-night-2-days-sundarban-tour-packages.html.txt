WHAT IS THE BEST TIME TO VISIT?             Unlike most National parks, The Sundarbans National Park is open through out the year. But maximum tourist visit the Sundarbans National Park between September to March. During this time the weather is pleasant and crocodiles are seen basking in the river banks. October to March is the best to enjoy Sundarban tour package due to the pleasant climate. But during the summers the chances of sighting of animals are higher as they come near the artificial water bodies adjoining the watchtowers.
IS It SAFE TO VISIT?             Sundarban is the world's largest mangrove forest, uniquely home to the Royal Bengal Tiger and abundant crocodiles. There has never been a reported tiger or crocodile attack on tourists. Forest entry requires a mandatory guide. Though visitors often ask about mosquitoes and insects, resorts and hotels provide repellents in every room, ensuring a comfortable stay.   ​

HOW To Reach FROM KOLKATA?
By Train- From Kolkata, take a train from Sealdah to Canning (1 hour), then travel by road to Godkhali (1 hour). From Godkhali, a 30-minute ferry takes you to Gosaba, followed by a 45-minute auto or van ride to Pakhirala Village, where various lodges and resorts are available.
By Road- There are two main routes to the Sundarbans. One goes via Baruipur–Canning, around 100 km from Kolkata, but takes nearly 4 hours due to heavy traffic. The other, via Basanti Highway, is longer in distance but quicker due to lighter traffic.
If you're heading to the Sundarbans via Godkhali, you'll be dropped there and continue to Pakhirala using the ferry and land route. However, if your destination is Jharkhali, you can drive straight there by car without needing a ferry.
WHAT IS SUNDARBANS FAMOUS FOR?
The Sundarbans is home to rare and threatened species like the Royal Bengal Tiger, estuarine crocodile, Gangetic dolphin, water monitor lizard, and olive ridley turtle. While wildlife sightings are possible, cruising on a houseboat with fresh seafood offers a truly immersive experience of nature at its purest.
WHAT ARE THE CHANCES OF SIGHTING TIGERS During The TRIP?
​​    The latest estimates show a rise in tiger numbers in the Indian Sundarbans, with the count reaching 96 in 2026, up from 88 in 2018–19. However, spotting a Royal Bengal Tiger in the wild remains rare—though not impossible.
