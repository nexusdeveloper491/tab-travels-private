FAQ | Frequently Asked Questions
Are same Day tour to Sundarban arranged on sharing basis?
One Day tour to Sundarbans or  Single day trip to Sundarban  are arranged as customized tours with shared Jungle safari by boat.
What is the timing for day trip to Sundarban?
A day tour starts at 5 am pickup is done from your place of stay in Kolkata and ends in late evening at around 8 pm.
What are included in an a day tour to Sundarbans?
Transportation from place of stay, breakfast, lunch, guided jungle safari is included in the packages. Two watchtowers are covered in the tour along with narrow creeks.
Is it possible to explore Sundarban in one day?
You can explore the part of National park and the backwaters of the Sundarbans delta and enjoy some great Bengali food.
WHAT IS THE BEST TIME TO VISIT?
Unlike most national parks, Sundarbans National Park remains open year-round. However, the peak tourist season is from September to March when the weather is pleasant and crocodiles can be seen basking on riverbanks. October to March is ideal for tours, but summer offers better chances of spotting animals near water bodies.
WHAT ARE THE PARK ENTRY CHARGES?
As of 01/01/2026, entry charges for a single-day Sundarbans tour covering Sajnekhali, Sudhanyakhali, and DoBanki Watch Towers are Rs 500 (Indians) and Rs 2000 (non-Indians) for boat entry and guide. Guide fees are Rs 700 for Indians and Rs 2000 for non-Indians. Local guides, have good jungle knowledge and speak Bengali and Hindi. For fluent English-speaking guides, separate booking is required.
​
WHAT ARE THE CHANCES OF SIGHTING TIGERS IN A TRIP?
The latest estimates show a rise in the tiger population in the Indian Sundarbans, with the count reaching 106 in 2026, up from 88 in 2018–19, according to the West Bengal Forest Department. However, spotting a Royal Bengal Tiger in the wild remains rare, though not impossible.

​​Sundarban 1 Day Tour Package Itinerary With Timings
​   ​​​​​​​    Detailed Itinerary Sundarban 1 One Day Tour Trip From Kolkata
5.00 Am: Single or 1(One) Day Tour To Sundarban starts from your hotel/ home by AC Car/Van. Three and half hours of drive to Godkhali, the last road point in Mainland.
8.30 Am: Board the boat, here you are welcomed by our crew with welcome drinks, breakfast and tea will be served on your cruise to reach Sajnekhali.
9.00 Am: After getting the Entry Permission & Guide we head off into the vast Sundarban forest for our rendezvous with one of the biggest mangroves of the world, rich in flora and fauna. This boat cruise during this period will visit 2 watch towers, namely Sajnekhali & Sudhanyakhali.
👉 Sajnekhali Watch Tower is most popular watch towers in the park. The reason behind its popularity is tourist can obtain the permission to enter the national park from this spot only. The major attractions are Bono Bini Temple, Mangrove Interpretation Center and turtle project.
This watch tower you can see avifauna species such as Pelican, Kingfishers, Plovers, Lap Wings, White Bellied Sea Eagle, Whimbrel, Curfews, and Sandpipers.
👉 Sudhanyakhali Watch Tower: Most of the tigers are sighted from this tower. Some other wildlife like deers, wild boars and crocodiles are commonly seen from this watch tower. There is a sweet water pond where animals come to drink water. Behind the pond are stretches of land devoid of any vegetation where one can sight animals in the clearings.
2.00 Pm: Lunch will be served during the cruise.
👉 Later  Cruising the small channels and creeks we will try to spot different wildlife, spotted dear, wild boars, civet cat, monitor lizard, crocodiles, dolphins and of course the tigers and diverse types of birds.
4.00 PM : Return to Godkhali the car and start for Kolkata.
8.00 PM : Reach Back to Kolkata Drop  at  Hotel/Home/Airport/Station. With this we end the  one day trip to Sundarban from kolkata .      ​     ​
