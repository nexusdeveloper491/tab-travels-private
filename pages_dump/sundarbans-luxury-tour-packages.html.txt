Key Highlights
Mother Nature, in all her grandeur, has sculpted a paradise straddling the borders of India and Bangladesh—the magnificent Sundarbans. Covering nearly 2,000 sq. km, this vast mangrove wonderland is one of the world’s last untouched wildernesses and the natural habitat of the legendary Royal Bengal Tiger. A designated UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Sundarbans is the largest river delta on Earth, formed by an intricate network of tidal rivers, creeks, and semi-submerged mangrove forests. Named after the abundant Sundari trees, it shelters an extraordinary range of wildlife, including over 260 species of birds, 120 varieties of fish, 8 species of amphibians, 50 species of reptiles, and India’s only known population of saltwater crocodiles.

What is the best time to visit Sundarbans?      ​​Unlike most national parks, Sundarbans National Park remains open throughout the year. However, the majority of tourists visit between September and March, when the weather is pleasant and crocodiles can often be seen basking on the riverbanks. The period from October to March is ideal for enjoying Sundarban luxury tour packages from Kolkata due to the favorable climate. In contrast, the summer months, though warmer, offer better chances of spotting wildlife as animals tend to gather around artificial water bodies near the watchtowers.

What are the chances of sighting tigers in Sundarban Trip Package?      ​  The latest estimates indicate a rise in the tiger population in the Indian Sundarbans. According to the West Bengal Forest Department, the number of Royal Bengal Tigers increased to 96 in 2019–20, up from 88 in 2018–19.
