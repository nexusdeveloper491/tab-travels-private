Which month is best for Sundarban?
Unlike most national parks in India, the Sundarbans National Park remains open throughout the year. However, the peak tourist season falls between September and March, when the weather is most pleasant. This is also the best time to witness crocodiles basking on the riverbanks and to enjoy scenic boat safaris under clear skies. Particularly, October to March is considered ideal for Sundarban tour packages due to the comfortable climate.
What is the cost of Sundarban tour 2 night 3 days package?

Exclusive curated packages for small or larger groups are organised with cost depending upon group size, quality of accommodation, food customization, etc.
How many days are enough for a Sundarban trip?
A 2 days / 1 night trip is ideal for most travelers—it covers transfers, a boat safari, village walk, and basic wildlife experience. If you want a more relaxed pace with deeper forest exploration and higher chances of spotting wildlife, go for 3 days / 2 nights. Longer stays are usually not necessary unless you’re a wildlife enthusiast or photographer.
Can I stay overnight in the Sundarban?
Yes, you can stay overnight in the Sundarban—but only in designated areas outside the core forest. Accommodation is available in eco-resorts and homestays on islands like Gosaba, Dayapur, and Bali. Staying inside the core forest is not allowed due to wildlife protection rules. Most tours include a safe and comfortable overnight stay along with meals and guided activities.
​  Can we see tigers in Sundarban?
The latest estimates indicate a rise in the population of Royal Bengal Tigers in the Indian Sundarbans. According to data from the West Bengal Forest Department, the tiger count increased to 96 in 2026, up from 88 in 2018–19. This growth is a positive sign for conservation efforts in the region.
However, if you're visiting the Sundarbans specifically to spot a tiger in the wild, you may need to temper your expectations. Tiger sightings are extremely rare due to the dense mangrove forest and the elusive nature of these big cats. That said, with some luck and patience, sightings are not impossible, especially during early mornings or low tides when animals come near the water’s edge.

Day 1 -  Departure To Sundarbans
8 Am: Depart from Kolkata in an air-conditioned car or passenger van/bus. Guests may also choose Science City as their pick-up point at 8:30 AM. A packed breakfast will be served onboard, and there will be a short stop for tea or coffee en route. The journey to Godkhali takes approximately three and a half hours — this marks the end of the road on the Indian mainland.
12 noon, board the boat and proceed to explore Gosaba Island, the largest marketplace serving hundreds of surrounding villages. The visit also includes stops at the historic Rabindranath Tagore Bungalow and the Hamilton Bungalow.
2.30 Pm: Reach resort/hotel on the island of Pakhiralay/ Dayapur by boat or by land, Check in at hotel followed by lunch. (Alternately we sometimes we do the Bird Sanctuary tour first then Check in at hotel). Later take a leisurely walk through scenic paddy fields and a quaint village inhabited by honey collectors and fishermen. Observe the unique local customs, rituals, and traditional beliefs that shape daily life here. The villages offer a rare glimpse into a simpler, rustic way of living.
4.30 Pm: Enjoy a serene two to three-hour boat ride dedicated to bird watching. Spot a variety of bird species including seven types of kingfishers, egrets, Brahminy kites, pond herons, black cormorants, and more. Along the way, get acquainted with the enchanting mangrove ecosystem, home to fascinating creatures such as fiddler crabs and mudskipper fish.
6:30 PM, return to the resort just as the sun sets. and by 7:00 PM, immerse yourself in a magical evening of tribal music and dance performed by local artists—you’re welcome to join in and celebrate with them. Refreshments will be served during the performance.
9:00 PM Dinner will be available from, served at your convenience, as you unwind and prepare for a comfortable night’s stay at our luxurious resort.
Day 2 -  Full Day  Jungle Safari
7.30 Am: We begin the day as early as possible by boarding a luxury boat. After obtaining the necessary permits and being joined by a forest-approved guide from the Forest Office, we set sail into the heart of one of the world’s largest and richest mangrove forests. This full-day boat cruise, lasting until sunset, offers an immersive experience into the unique ecosystem of the Sundarbans.
9 am Breakfast is served on cruise. During the journey, we will visit three prominent watchtowers—Sajnekhali, Sudhanyakhali, and Dobanki. Highlights of the day include the Sajnekhali Tiger Project, Crocodile and Turtle Conservation Projects, the Mangrove Interpretation Centre, the revered Bono Bibi Temple, the Dobanki Canopy Walk, and scenic spots like Pirkhali, Gazikhali, Pancha Mukhani (the confluence of five rivers), and various narrow creeks and channels.
2pm Lunch is served on cruise. As we glide through these serene waterways, we’ll keep a lookout for a variety of wildlife, including spotted deer, wild boars, civet cats, monitor lizards, crocodiles, dolphins, and with luck, the elusive Royal Bengal Tiger. Tea/Coffee and refreshmets are served during the cruise.
Return to resort/hotel by 5 to 6 pm in the evening, later enjoy the evening at leisure or enjoy a campfire(during winters) or enjoy chicken Bar B Que. Dinner is served at 9 pm.
Day 3 -  Return Back To Kolkata
Begin your day early with a peaceful morning walk through the village—an ideal time to capture stunning photographs of the sunrise, mangroves, village life, and local birds. For those who prefer a bit more adventure, bicycles are available to explore the island on wheels.
8:00 AM onwards, breakfast will be served at the resort or hotel. Afterward, we will begin our return journey by boat to Godkhali. A freshly prepared lunch will be served onboard starting around 12 noon.
2:00 and 2:30 PM We are expected to reach Godkhali and start drive back towards Kolkata.
5:30 PM We reach Kolkata and with that we end the 2 Night 3 Days Sundarban Tour Package. ​
