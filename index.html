The Sundarbans delta is an enchanting world of tidal waterways, breathing mangrove roots, and rich Royal Bengal Tiger lore. Having guided over 25,000+ delighted guests from across India and the globe, we combine raw wildlife exploration with genuine Bengali hospitality, licensed safari cruisers, handpicked island eco-resorts in Pakhiralay & Dayapur Island, and freshly prepared onboard feasts.

WILDLIFE SAFARI GUIDE

Key Watchtowers & Sightseeing Points

Strategic observation towers inside the core tiger reserve offering elevated vantage points over freshwater drinking ponds and mangrove channels.

Forest Permits & Museum

Sajnekhali Watch Tower

Entry hub of the Sundarbans. Houses the Mangrove Interpretation Centre, Crocodile breeding pond, Olive Ridley turtle hatchery, and sweet water pond with birdwatching hide.

Prime Tiger Spotting Zone

Sudhanyakhali Watch Tower

The most famous tower for tiger encounters. Overlooks a vast sweet-water pond surrounded by fortified fencing where spotted deer, wild boars, and monitors gather.

Elevated 20ft Canopy Walk

Dobanki Canopy Walk

Features a unique 500-meter elevated mesh canopy walk 20 feet above ground level, allowing travelers to walk through dense mangrove crowns with panoramic views.

Narrow Creek Safari

Pirkhali & Gazikhali Creeks

Thrilling narrow water channels flanked by aerial pneumatophore roots. Excellent chances of spotting saltwater crocodiles basking on mud banks, Kingfishers, and dolphins.

Gosaba Heritage

Hamilton & Tagore Bungalow

Explore the heritage wooden bungalow of Sir Daniel Hamilton (established India’s first cooperative estate) and where Rabindranath Tagore stayed during his Sundarban tour.

Folk Lore & Mythology

Bono Bibi & Dakshin Rai

Learn about the revered forest deity Bonobibi—the guardian angel worshiped equally by Hindu and Muslim honey collectors and fishermen before entering the tiger territory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sundarban tour packages, booking process, safety, and wildlife sightings.

The cost of a Sundarban tour package depends on duration, departure point, and comfort level. Standard group packages from Kolkata for 2 Nights / 3 Days range between ₹5,500 (Non-AC) to ₹6,500 (Deluxe AC) per person. 1 Night / 2 Days packages start at ₹4,000 – ₹4,500. Premium luxury packages range from ₹7,500 to ₹12,000+. All packages include AC road transfers, boat safari, resort stays, all meals, forest permits, and guide charges.

Sundarban National Park remains open throughout the year. The most popular time is between October and March when the weather is cool and sunny, and crocodiles frequently bask on river mudbanks. During the monsoon (July to September), the lush greenery is breathtaking and our famous Sundarban Ilish Utsav (Hilsa Festival) takes place.

The most comfortable way is by road in our pre-arranged AC vehicle from Kolkata (pickup point at Science City / selected hubs). The distance to Godkhali port is around 90–100 km (approx. 3 hours). At Godkhali, you board our dedicated safari cruiser to enter the mangrove delta and reach your resort island in Gosaba / Pakhiralay.

The Sundarbans has a healthy population of Royal Bengal Tigers. Since mangroves are dense, tiger sightings require patience and luck, frequently occurring near water channels and sweet water ponds at Sudhanyakhali and Dobanki. You will also see spotted deer, crocodiles, wild boars, monitor lizards, dolphins, and diverse exotic kingfishers.
